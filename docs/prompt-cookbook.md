# Prompt cookbook

Copy these into Claude Code or Codex and change the details. You don't need
exact wording. These are just starting points.

## Starting out

> I want to make a video.

The AI asks for a name, creates that project and opens its **assets/** folder
for your content. You can also supply the name immediately:

> Create a project called "Bakery Launch" for Instagram Reels and TikTok.

> My files are in the folder now. Check what I added and help me write the brief.

> Help me plan a video. Ask me what it's about, who it's for and the tone,
> then write the brief and a script.

To see what you have already made:

> Show me my projects.

## Photo slideshow

> Make a 20-second slideshow from the photos in ~/Pictures/wedding.
> Gentle zoom on each photo, soft fades between them, the couple's names
> on the first slide and the date on the last. Use the song in
> ~/Music/our-song.mp3, faded out at the end.

## Talking head with captions

> I've got a 45-second clip of me talking: ~/Desktop/tip.mov.
> Transcribe it and add big bold captions, one or two words at a time,
> highlighted as I speak. Put a title card at the start: "3 tips for
> better sleep". Make it for TikTok and Reels.

## Product promo

> Make a 15-second promo for our new candle. Photos are in
> ~/Downloads/candle. Scenes: product reveal, three quick benefits
> ("hand-poured", "40-hour burn", "100% soy"), then the price and
> "Shop now at example.com". Earthy colours, elegant serif font.

## Quote or announcement card

> Make a 6-second animated quote card: "The best time to start was
> yesterday. The next best time is now." Dark background, words
> appearing one by one. Square for Instagram and LinkedIn.

## Event recap

> Here are clips from our meetup: ~/Downloads/meetup. Make a 30-second
> high-energy recap. Pick the best moments, quick cuts on the beat of
> ~/Music/upbeat.mp3, our logo (~/Desktop/logo.png) at the end.

## Turning one video into every format

> Add YouTube, Instagram square and LinkedIn versions of this project.
> Rearrange the layout so it looks good in each shape, then show me each
> one in Studio.

> Export all formats.

## Steering in Studio

> The headline is hidden behind TikTok's buttons. Move it up.

> Everything feels rushed. Make the whole video 5 seconds longer and slow
> the transitions down.

> Swap the second and third scenes.

> Use the brand colour #2E7D5B for all headings.

> Keep all titles and captions inside the red safe-area guide in every format.

## Fixing things

> The captions say "remote shun" instead of "Remotion". Fix it.

> The export failed. What went wrong?

> Undo the last change.

## Housekeeping

> Open the exports folder for Bakery Launch.

> What files are in this project?

> Set up my OpenAI key for transcription.
